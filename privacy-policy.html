Privacy Policy

How Health Tracker stores and uses the information you choose to record.

In short: your health entries are stored on your device. Health Tracker does not send them to MicroAppTechnology.

Information in the app

Health Tracker stores the readings and details you enter, including blood pressure, heart rate, blood sugar, notes, and reminder settings. The app does not require an online account. These entries are stored locally on your device.

How your information is used

Your entries are used to show your logs, charts, summaries, and reminders in the app. You can create a backup file or restore records from a backup in Settings. Backup copies are saved to Downloads and remain under your control.

Sharing and service providers

Health entries are not transmitted to MicroAppTechnology and are not shared by the app with advertising or analytics providers. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Android and Google Play provide platform services used by the app. Those services may process technical information under their own privacy terms.

Permissions and connectivity

If you enable reminders, Android notifications are scheduled on your device. The optional in-app update check uses Google Play Core. During an update check, Google Play may process device and app technical information to determine whether an update is available. This check does not send your health entries.

You can manage notification permissions in Android settings. You can use the app without enabling reminders.

Retention and deletion

Your entries remain on your device until you delete them, clear Health Tracker's app data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove entries that have not been backed up. Backup copies remain wherever you save or share them until you delete them.

Children

Health Tracker is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. For privacy questions, email microappstechnology@gmail.com.

Health notice

Health Tracker is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. It records values you enter manually and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Discuss readings with a qualified healthcare professional. Do not rely on the app for urgent or emergency care.
