How Health Tracker:BP Monitor App stores and uses the information you choose to record.

In short: your health entries are stored on your device. Health Tracker:BP Monitor App does not send them to MicroAppTechnology.

Information in the app

Health Tracker:BP Monitor App stores the readings and details you enter, such as blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate, weight, BMI, notes, and reminder settings. The app does not require an online account. These entries are stored locally on your device.

How your information is used

Your entries are used to show your logs, charts, summaries, and reminders in the app. You can choose to export or back up your information. An exported or backup file is created for you to save or share; once you share it, the recipient and any service you choose to use may handle that copy under their own terms.

Sharing and service providers

Health entries are not transmitted to MicroAppTechnology and are not shared by the app with advertising or analytics providers. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Android and Google Play provide platform services used by the app. Those services may process technical information under their own privacy terms.

Permissions and connectivity

If you enable reminders, Android notifications are scheduled on your device. Health Tracker:BP Monitor App automatically checks for updates through Google Play Core when its main screen opens. For this check, Google Play processes device metadata, the app version, and the list of installed app modules or asset packs to determine whether an update is available and its expected size. Google states this information is encrypted in transit, is not transferred to other third parties, and is deleted after a fixed retention period. The update check does not send your health entries to Google Play or MicroAppTechnology.

You can manage notification permissions in Android settings. You can use the app without enabling reminders.

Retention and deletion

Your entries remain on your device until you delete them, clear Health Tracker:BP Monitor App's app data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove entries that have not been exported. Exported and backup copies remain wherever you save or share them until you delete those copies.

Children

Health Tracker:BP Monitor App is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. For privacy questions, email microappstechnology@gmail.com.

Health notice

Health Tracker:BP Monitor App is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. It records values you enter manually and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Discuss readings with a qualified healthcare professional. Do not rely on the app for urgent or emergency care.
